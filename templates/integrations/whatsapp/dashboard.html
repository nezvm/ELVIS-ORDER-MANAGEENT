Quick Setup Guide

  1. 1 Create a new Business Portfolio in Meta Business Suite (separate from Elvis Co)
  2. 2 Create a Meta App at developers.facebook.com under the new portfolio
  3. 3 Add WhatsApp product to your app and configure the webhook above
  4. 4 Set up Embedded Signup configuration in WhatsApp → Getting Started
  5. 5 Click "Connect Number" and select your Elvis Co portfolio to connect numbers
Tip: The app must be owned by a different portfolio than Elvis Co for Embedded Signup to work.
