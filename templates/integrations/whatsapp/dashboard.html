Setup Instructions

📍 Navigate to: developers.facebook.com → Your App → WhatsApp → Configuration

  1. 1 Go to developers.facebook.com/apps
  2. 2 Create App → Select "Other" → Then "Business" type
  3. 3 In App Dashboard, find "WhatsApp" card → Click "Set Up"
  4. 4 Left sidebar: Click WhatsApp → Configuration
  5. 5 Scroll to "Webhook" section → Click "Edit"
  6. 6 Paste Callback URL and Verify Token from left → Click "Verify and Save"
  7. 7 Under "Webhook fields" → Click "Manage" → Toggle "messages" to Subscribe

📱 For Coexistence (Keep using WhatsApp Business App):

  1. Step 1: Go to business.facebook.com/wa/manage
  2. Step 2: Click "Phone Numbers" → "+ Add Phone Number"
  3. Step 3: Select "Use existing WhatsApp Business App number"
  4. Step 4: Choose "Enable Coexistence" → QR code will appear!
  5. Step 5: On phone: Open WhatsApp Business → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device → Scan QR

⚠️ Requires: WA Business App v2.24.17+, number active 7+ days, supported country

Common Mistake: The "messages" field is ONLY under WhatsApp → Configuration. It will NOT appear under Facebook Login or Instagram webhooks!
