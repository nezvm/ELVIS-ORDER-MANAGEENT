Data Deletion Instructions

How to request deletion of your data

Your Data Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to request deletion of your personal data. We are committed to honoring your data deletion requests promptly.

What Data Can Be Deleted

You can request deletion of:

  • Your account and login information
  • WhatsApp lead data associated with your business
  • Message history and conversation logs
  • Customer contact information stored in our system
  • Any other personal data we hold about you

How to Request Data Deletion

1 Email Request

Send an email to our data protection team with your deletion request:

Email: admin@elvisbeauty.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Please include:

  • Your full name
  • Email address associated with your account
  • WhatsApp Business phone number (if applicable)
  • Specific data you want deleted (or "all data")

2 In-App Request (For Account Holders)

If you have an active account, you can request data deletion from within the platform:

  1. Log in to your Elvis Manager account
  2. Go to Settings → Account Settings
  3. Click on "Delete My Data" or "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your request

3 Facebook/Meta Login Users

If you connected via Facebook/Meta login, you can also revoke access:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites
  2. Find "WALS" or "Elvis Manager" in the list
  3. Click "Remove" to revoke access
  4. This will trigger automatic data deletion on our end

What Happens After Your Request

  • Processing Time: We will process your request within 30 days
  • Confirmation: You will receive email confirmation once deletion is complete
  • Backup Removal: Data may persist in backups for up to 90 days before complete removal
  • Legal Retention: Some data may be retained if required by law

Data We Cannot Delete

Certain data may be retained for legal or legitimate business reasons:

  • Transaction records required for tax/accounting purposes
  • Data needed for legal claims or disputes
  • Aggregated, anonymized data that cannot identify you
  • Data shared with WhatsApp/Meta (subject to their policies)

Contact Us
